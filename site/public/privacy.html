Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

Ely Office is a set of desktop apps (ElyDoc, ElyTable, ElyShow, ElyPDF, ElyTree, ElyMark, ElyMail and ElyAgent) made by Yang Zhang (“we”). The apps work on your computer. We do not run servers that receive your files, mail or account data, and we do not collect analytics or telemetry.

What stays on your computer

  • Your documents stay where you save them. The apps never upload them.
  • Settings, recent files, recovery copies, logs and the mail database are kept in the app's data folder on your computer. You can clear them in Settings › Storage, or delete the folder.
  • Passwords, OAuth tokens and recovery keys are stored in your operating system's credential store (Keychain, Windows Credential Manager or Secret Service).
  • Diagnostics are exported to a file only when you ask, and are never sent anywhere by the apps.

When the apps use the network

Only for things you ask for, and only directly between your computer and the service involved:

  • Update checks. Off by default. When you turn them on or choose “Check for Updates…”, the app asks GitHub for the latest release. GitHub sees your IP address.
  • ElyMail. Connects to the mail servers of the accounts you add (IMAP, POP3, SMTP) to fetch, organise and send your mail. Remote images in messages are blocked until you choose to load them; loading them lets the sender's server see your IP address.
  • ElyTree. Fetch, pull, push and clone contact the Git remotes you configure.
  • ElyAgent. Sends your prompts and the context you attach to the AI provider you sign in to. That provider's own privacy policy applies.

Google and Microsoft accounts

ElyMail can sign in to Gmail and Microsoft accounts with OAuth. Microsoft accounts are handled the same way as Google accounts below.

Google user data ElyMail accesses

  • Gmail (https://mail.google.com/). Your mail folders, messages, attachments and flags, and the ability to send mail, over IMAP, POP3 and SMTP. This is the only scope Gmail accepts for these protocols.
  • Basic profile (openid, email). Your email address, used to name the account.

How ElyMail uses it

Only to provide the mail features you see in the app: show, search and organise your mail; mark, move, archive and delete the messages you choose; keep your drafts and Sent copies; and send the mail you write. ElyMail never sends mail, deletes mail or changes your account on its own.

Sharing

ElyMail talks only to Google's servers, directly from your computer. We have no server, and we never receive, see or store your Google data or tokens. Your Google data is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, not used to train AI or machine-learning models, and not sent to ElyAgent or any AI service. No person other than you can read it through Ely Office.

Storage and protection

Tokens are kept in your operating system's credential store. Mail is kept in ElyMail's local database in the app's data folder on your computer. All connections to Google use TLS.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your computer until you delete it. Removing the account in ElyMail deletes its token and all its local mail. You can also revoke ElyMail's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing the account never deletes mail on Google's servers.

Limited Use

ElyMail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

office.elygpui.com is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes requests (such as your IP address) to serve the site. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It remembers your light or dark choice in your browser's local storage. Downloads are served by GitHub.

Children

Ely Office is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anyone's data.

Changes

We will post any change on this page and update the date above.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/ELY-Open-Source/ElyOffice.

隐私政策

2026 年 10 月 20 日起生效

Ely Office 是 Yang Zhang（“我们”）制作的一组桌面应用（ElyDoc、ElyTable、ElyShow、ElyPDF、ElyTree、ElyMark、ElyMail 与 ElyAgent），在你的电脑上运行。我们没有接收你的文件、邮件或账号数据的服务器，也不收集分析或遥测数据。

留在你电脑上的

  • 文档留在你保存的位置，应用从不上传。
  • 设置、最近文件、恢复副本、日志与邮件数据库存在本机的应用数据文件夹里，可在“设置›存储”清除，或直接删除该文件夹。
  • 密码、OAuth 令牌与恢复密钥存在系统凭据库（钥匙串、Windows 凭据管理器或 Secret Service）。
  • 诊断只在你要求时导出为文件，应用从不发送。

应用何时联网

只在你要求时，且只在你的电脑与相应服务之间直接进行：

  • 检查更新。默认关闭。打开后或选“检查更新…”时，应用向 GitHub 询问最新版本，GitHub 会看到你的 IP 地址。
  • ElyMail。连接你添加的邮箱的服务器（IMAP、POP3、SMTP）收取、整理与发送邮件。邮件里的远程图片默认不载入；你选择载入时，发件方的服务器会看到你的 IP 地址。
  • ElyTree。取回、拉取、推送与克隆连接你设置的 Git 远程。
  • ElyAgent。把你的提示与附带的内容发给你登录的 AI 服务商，适用其隐私政策。

Google 与 Microsoft 账号

ElyMail 可用 OAuth 登录 Gmail 与 Microsoft 账号。Microsoft 账号的处理方式与下文的 Google 账号相同。

ElyMail 访问的 Google 用户数据

  • Gmail（https://mail.google.com/）。经 IMAP、POP3、SMTP 访问你的邮件文件夹、邮件、附件与标记，并发送邮件。Gmail 对这些协议只接受这一权限。
  • 基本资料（openid、email）。你的邮箱地址，用于命名账号。

用途

只用于你在应用里看到的邮件功能：显示、搜索与整理邮件；对你选中的邮件标记、移动、归档与删除；保存草稿与已发送副本；发送你写的邮件。ElyMail 从不自行发信、删信或改动你的账号。

共享

ElyMail 只从你的电脑直接连接 Google 的服务器。我们没有服务器，从不接收、查看或存储你的 Google 数据与令牌。你的 Google 数据不出售，不与任何人共享，不用于广告，不用于训练 AI 或机器学习模型，也不发给 ElyAgent 或任何 AI 服务。除你之外，没有人能经 Ely Office 读到它。

存储与保护

令牌存在系统凭据库，邮件存在你电脑上应用数据文件夹里的 ElyMail 本地数据库。与 Google 的连接一律经 TLS 加密。

保留与删除

数据留在你的电脑上，直到你删除。在 ElyMail 里移除账号即删除其令牌与全部本地邮件。你也可随时在 myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤销 ElyMail 的访问权限。移除账号从不删除 Google 服务器上的邮件。

有限使用

ElyMail 对从 Google API 获得的信息的使用及向任何其他应用的传输，将遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其“有限使用”要求。

本网站

office.elygpui.com 托管在 Cloudflare，为提供网站会处理请求（如你的 IP 地址）。网站不设 Cookie，不用分析工具，只在浏览器本地存储里记住你选的深浅色。下载由 GitHub 提供。

儿童

Ely Office 不面向儿童，我们不会有意收集任何人的数据。

变更
