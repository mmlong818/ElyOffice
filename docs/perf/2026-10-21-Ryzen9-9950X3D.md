# Benchmark 2026-10-21

Format: docs/perf/README.md. ElyMail frame times (C12.23, 120 fps), on Windows, by hand: `bench.py` drives macOS only.

The release build has `--features frames`; with `ELY_FRAMES=1` it logs, every 2 s with drawing, gpui's journal of presented frames: time inside `Window::draw` and from the first invalidation to the platform present. `ELY_DATA_ROOT` points at a seeded folder: 20,000 plain-text messages in one inbox (`seeds_a_data_root_for_frame_timing` in `crates/ely-mail-engine/tests/search_perf.rs`), an account with no server, never the user's data. Input came from `SendInput` (wheel) and `SendKeys` (typing), the window in front throughout.

| Case | Frames | Draw P50 ms | Draw P95 ms | Draw max ms | Dirty to shown P95 ms | Dirty to shown max ms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| List scroll: 80 wheel notches down, 80 up, 25 ms apart | 161 | 2.4 to 2.7 | 2.9 to 3.3 | 3.8 | 17.0 to 18.8 | 19.0 |
| Typing 36 characters in the search box, 60 ms apart | 65 | 1.5 to 2.7 | 1.9 to 2.9 | 3.3 | 10.6 to 18.1 | 19.6 |
| Typing 87 characters in a Markdown message body | 100 | 1.2 to 1.5 | 1.3 to 2.8 | 3.3 | 12.0 to 17.2 | 18.0 |

Ranges are across the 2 s log lines. Every frame's draw fit in a 120 fps frame (8.3 ms) with room: the slowest was 3.8 ms. Dirty to shown includes waiting for the display's vertical sync; on this 59 Hz display that wait reaches about 17 ms, so these numbers cannot show 120 fps presentation. A 120 Hz display and the Raspberry Pi were not measured.
